  • Compact Spacing
  • Sidebar Auto-close
  • Toggle + Title Horizontal

📐 Ukuran Mobile

  • Grid: 3 kolom, gap 0.5rem
  • Card padding: 0.625rem
  • Font H3: 0.7rem
  • Icon: 28px × 28px
  • Toggle: 2.25rem × 2.25rem

📱 Small Mobile (≤480px)

  • Responsive window resize
